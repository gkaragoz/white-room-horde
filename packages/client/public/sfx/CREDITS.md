- `ui_*.ogg`: "Interface Sounds" by Kenney, https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds (CC0 1.0)
- `footstep_concrete_*.ogg`: "Impact Sounds" by Kenney (CC0 1.0)
- `../music/determined_pursuit.ogg` (converted from WAV to Vorbis, 80 kbps): "Determined Pursuit (Epic Orchestra Loop)" by Emma_MA, https://opengameart.org/content/determined-pursuit-epic-orchestra-loop (CC0, public domain)

- `gun_<weapon>_<n>.*` (pistol, revolver, mp, smg, ar, shotgun, dbl, hmg, minigun, sniper, dmr): single shots cut out of the recordings in "The Free Firearm Sound Library" by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney (CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library): 1911, Smith & Wesson 642, Walther PPQ, Carl Gustav M45, AR-15, Benelli Nova, Mossberg 190, AK-47, PPSh, Mosin Nagant and Norinco SKS. Cut, soft-clipped and converted by `tools/audio/make_guns.py`.
- `gun_flamer_*`, `gun_gl_*`: synthesised for this game by `tools/audio/make_guns.py` (filtered noise and sine sweeps).
- `reload_pistol.*`, `reload_rifle.*`, `reload_shotgun.*`: "Gun reload sounds" by SpringySpringo (CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds).
- `floor_cleared`, `count_tick`, `count_go`, `floor_charge`, `floor_lift`, `floor_arrive`, `floor_start` (the climb between floors): cut, layered, pitch-shaped and normalised by `tools/audio/make_floor_sfx.py` from "Sci-fi Sounds" by Kenney (CC0 1.0, https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds: forceField, spaceEngine, thrusterFire, lowFrequency_explosion) and "63 digital sound effects" by Kenney (CC0 1.0, https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc: powerUp, pepSound, lowThreeTone).
